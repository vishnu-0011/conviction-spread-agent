Prove the broker before the strategy

Phase 1 answers a deliberately boring but decisive question: does the actual development paper account expose everything our spread agent assumes?

Tangible win: produce a masked capability report proving account health, Level 3 permission, contract discovery, and option snapshot access—without sending a single order request.

The four boundaries

1 · AccountActive, unblocked, Level 3, buying power.
2 · SessionBroker clock, open state, next open and close.
3 · ContractsTradable SPY options in an explicit DTE window.
4 · DataIndicative snapshots, quotes, nullable Greeks and IV.

Documentation describes the platform in general. It cannot prove the permissions, subscription, or response fields of our specific account. That is why this phase is an integration test rather than more strategy design.

Your part

  1. Use a development paper account—not the fresh final judging account.
  2. Copy .env.example to .env.
  3. Add the paper API key and secret locally. Never paste either into chat.
  4. Keep paper mode true, execution disabled, and dry-run true.

# Edit .env locally, then:
python scripts/preflight.py --underlying SPY `
  --output data/private/preflight.json
Safety boundary: the preflight source contains GET requests only. It cannot submit, replace, cancel, exercise, or close an order. Its saved report is gitignored and masks both account identifiers.

What passing means

  • The credentials authenticate against the paper host.
  • The account is active and neither trading-blocked nor account-blocked.
  • The configured options level is 3 or higher.
  • Options buying power is present and positive.
  • Tradable SPY contracts exist between 14 and 35 DTE.
  • Indicative option snapshots return successfully.

Greeks and implied volatility are informative but non-critical in this check. Alpaca documents them as nullable, so missing values activate our fallback rather than becoming fake zeroes.

Retrieval check

Why do we use a development account first?

Experiments and mistakes must not pollute the fresh account whose activity judges will evaluate.

Why is Level 3 a release gate?

Our bull-call and bear-put spreads are multi-leg strategies; lower permission levels cannot support the intended atomic MLeg execution.

Why is a successful snapshot not proof of realistic execution?

Basic access uses Alpaca Indicative data, while paper fills are simulated. We must label the feed and avoid treating paper results as live execution evidence.

What I build after it passes

I will convert the observed responses into typed Alpaca adapters, add recorded fixtures for missing Greeks and sparse quotes, and create a deterministic chain selector. Order submission will remain disabled.
