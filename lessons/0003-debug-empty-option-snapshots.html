Debug empty option snapshots

A partial preflight failure is evidence. Read which boundary failed before changing accounts, subscriptions, or code.

Tangible win: distinguish the brokerage control plane from the market-data plane and isolate an empty response without placing an order.

What the first report proved

AuthPassed
Level 3Passed
ContractsPassed
SnapshotsFailed

Because authentication, permissions, and contract discovery passed, regenerating keys or changing account settings would have addressed the wrong boundary.

Competing explanations

  1. Market timing: the check ran at 7:00 a.m. ET, before options open.
  2. Feed access: the account might lack Indicative entitlement.
  3. Contract sampling: the first metadata records might not have usable quotes.

The read-only experiment

The diagnostic fetched SPY's IEX reference price, limited strikes to ±5%, ranked contracts by distance from spot, and compared three official data surfaces.

Chain snapshots:       462
Selected snapshots:     14 / 20
Latest quotes:           14 / 20
Snapshots with Greeks:   14 / 14
Snapshots with IV:       14 / 14
Market open:             false

Data arrived before the market opened, so timing and entitlement were not the cause. Near-money selection worked; taking the first ten metadata records did not.

The correction

  1. Get the underlying IEX snapshot.
  2. Use the broker timestamp to derive the trading date.
  3. Constrain strikes to 95–105% of the underlying price.
  4. Rank tradable contracts by absolute distance from spot.
  5. Probe twenty contracts rather than assuming the first ten are useful.
General lesson: “tradable” is a contract/account property. It does not guarantee a current quote, a tight market, Greeks, or a good execution candidate.

Retrieval check

Why did we avoid regenerating API keys?

Authentication and all account checks already passed; the failure was downstream in market-data selection.

What observation ruled out market hours as the root cause?

Near-money snapshots, quotes, Greeks, and IV returned while the broker clock still reported the market closed.

What does an active, tradable contract fail to guarantee?

It does not guarantee a current quote, liquidity, Greeks, or a suitable spread leg.
