When will Alpaca show the actual output?

Separate live market data, shadow decisions, paper orders, and broker-confirmed P&L. They are four different proof levels.

Short answer: live Alpaca market data is available now. Orders, positions, and paper P&L will appear only after the deterministic execution state machine and autonomous Alpaca integration are complete and we arm a one-contract canary order.

The four proof levels

1 · FixtureComplete: local research simulation
2 · Live dataAvailable: read-only Alpaca quotes
3 · ShadowNext: live decision, no order
4 · Paper tradeLater: broker order, fill, P&L

What we can see today

python scripts/preflight.py --underlying SPY

This uses the Alpaca paper credentials in the local .env and retrieves account capability, broker clock, contracts, and near-money option snapshots. That is real broker and market-data output, but it is not a strategy trade.

What is still missing

  1. Complete live spread selection and deterministic sizing.
  2. Complete the submit → acknowledge → fill → reconcile → close state machine.
  3. Connect the scheduled agent through Alpaca MCP or CLI.
  4. Run shadow mode first, where a live decision cannot submit an order.
  5. Explicitly arm a one-contract paper canary after shadow evidence passes.

What appears after the canary is armed

  • The accepted or rejected order in the Alpaca paper account
  • Order updates and simulated fills from Alpaca
  • The resulting option legs under paper positions
  • Updated simulated balance and unrealized or realized P&L
  • A local decision record linked to the broker order ID

Alpaca documents paper trading as a real-time simulation: orders are matched using current quotes rather than routed to an exchange. The web dashboard can display the simulated activity and balance. Options positions and balances update immediately, although some non-trade activity records may appear the following day.

Best time for the first options canary

Use a normal US trading day during regular market hours: 9:30 a.m.–4:00 p.m. ET. During US daylight-saving time, that is 7:00 p.m.–1:30 a.m. in India. A clean demo should avoid the opening minutes, the closing window, stale quotes, and event risk.

Hackathon meaning of “live”: real-time Alpaca paper trading, not real-money trading. We should not enable a real brokerage account for this project.
