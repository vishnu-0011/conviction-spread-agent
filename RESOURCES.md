# ConvictionSpread Learning Resources

## Knowledge

- [Alpaca: How to Start Paper Trading](https://alpaca.markets/learn/start-paper-trading)
  Current first-party click path for selecting a paper account, generating API keys,
  and verifying a connection. Use for: initial development-account setup.
- [Alpaca: Paper Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading)
  First-party explanation of the simulator and its differences from live execution.
  Use for: interpreting paper fills and describing limitations to judges.
- [Alpaca: Options Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-trading)
  First-party overview of permissions, buying power, exercise, and options levels.
  Use for: account preflight and lifecycle rules.
- [Alpaca: Level 3 Options Trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/options-level-3-trading)
  Current MLeg structure and restrictions. Use for: debit-spread entry and exit orders.
- [Alpaca: Market Data Plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)
  Documents Indicative versus OPRA access and plan limits. Use for: feed labeling,
  data-quality assumptions, and rate-aware design.
- [Alpaca API: Option Snapshots](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optionsnapshots)
  Latest trade, quote, and Greeks for selected contracts. Use for: targeted data probes.
- [Alpaca API: Option Chain](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/optionchain)
  Underlying-wide option snapshots with strike and expiration filters. Use for: chain
  discovery and comparison with selected-contract responses.
- [Alpaca: Trading MCP Server](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/alpaca-mcp-server)
  Official MCP setup and capabilities. Use for: the required hackathon integration.
- [Local: Alpaca platform research](docs/research/alpaca-platform-research.md)
  Our cited, project-specific synthesis. Use for: architecture and validation decisions.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Alpaca Community Forum](https://forum.alpaca.markets/)
  Active official user forum with Alpaca staff participation. Use for: reproducing API
  behavior, reporting paper/options discrepancies, and comparing integration findings.

## Gaps

- The organizers' authoritative policy on implementation completed before August 28.
- Whether the event provides OPRA entitlement or a structured earnings calendar.
- The exact submission form interpretation of account UUID versus account number.
