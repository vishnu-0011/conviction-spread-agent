# Mission: Build and defend ConvictionSpread

## Why
Build a credible autonomous options agent for the lablab.ai × Alpaca hackathon, while
learning the trading, API, risk, and evaluation concepts well enough to explain every
decision to judges rather than presenting an opaque bot.

## Success looks like
- Explain the complete market-data → thesis → spread → risk → execution lifecycle.
- Safely inspect and operate an Alpaca paper account without exposing credentials.
- Implement and test a bounded-risk autonomous options strategy.
- Present public engineering evidence, honest results, and limitations to judges.

## Constraints
- The event runs for seven days and requires options plus Alpaca MCP or CLI.
- All execution remains in paper trading; the final judging account must be fresh.
- Development progress should be committed and pushed after meaningful tasks.
- Prefer short, hands-on lessons connected directly to the current build phase.

## Out of scope
- Real-money trading or live brokerage deployment.
- High-frequency trading and latency-arbitrage infrastructure.
- A general-purpose library covering every options strategy.
